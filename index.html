Kriox

Supply that never melts.

Kriox is a prototype purchasing and goods-receipt assistant for an ice-cream manufacturer. It watches stock and sales, prepares purchase requisitions and orders, asks suppliers for quotes, checks delivery documents against the order, and leaves every consequential decision to a named person in an approval queue.

How Kriox uses Gmail

Suppliers answer purchase orders and price requests by e-mail. Kriox reads the replies that arrive at its own address in one connected Gmail inbox, links each reply to the order it belongs to, and shows it to the people responsible. It does not keep any other mail in that inbox. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
